Privacy policy

JEG Trading, Curaçao and Aruba · Effective 13 November 2026

This policy explains what the JEG Trading business system (the website, customer portal, dashboard and kiosk) does with information, including the information it reads from QuickBooks Online.

1. What we read from QuickBooks Online

When JEG Trading connects its own QuickBooks Online company, the system reads only what it needs to run orders and stock:

  • Products and services: id, name, SKU, type, quantity on hand, sales price.
  • Customers: id, display name, email, open balance.
  • Open invoices: id, number, customer, total, balance, due date.

The connection uses Intuit's own sign in (OAuth 2.0). We never ask for, see or store a QuickBooks username or password.

2. What we store

  • The access key Intuit issues for the connection, encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in JEG Trading's own database, readable only by the server.
  • The QuickBooks company id and the time of the last sync.
  • Orders and contact details customers enter in the portal or website.

QuickBooks records themselves are read when needed and shown on screen. They are not copied into a separate database.

3. No AI training

Data from QuickBooks Online, and any data derived from it, is never used to train, fine tune or develop any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, by us or by anyone we work with.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Vercel (hosting of the website and server functions).
  • Supabase (the encrypted connection record and portal data).
  • Intuit (QuickBooks Online itself).
  • Optimize Your Vibe, the team that builds and maintains the system for JEG Trading, under contract.

We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share QuickBooks data for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

When QuickBooks is disconnected, the stored key is cancelled at Intuit and deleted from our database. Portal orders are kept as long as needed for bookkeeping and the law in Curaçao and Aruba.

6. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Keys are encrypted at rest, kept server side only, and the database is locked so the public site cannot read it.

7. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or disconnect QuickBooks at any time, by writing to the address below.
